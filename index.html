Botellón
boh · teh · YON noun, Spanish

Literally, "big bottle." In Spain, a botellón is when friends gather in the plaza to share a bottle, some food and a good time. That's the spirit we built Botellon on.

Tip: the double "ll" sounds like a "y", and the stress lands on the last syllable.

The Menu

Made to share. As a guide, we recommend two smaller dishes and one main between two. We source our ingredients as locally as possible and everything is made fresh to order. Our classics stay all year round, and the rest of the menu changes with the seasons.

Dietary needs? Check the tags on each dish below before you visit, and our team is happy to help on the night. Our menu is built around seafood, meat and cheese, so we're limited for vegan diners, but we're always happy to help where we can.

Please always tell our team about any dietaries or allergies when you book and when you order, especially if they're serious. Our kitchen handles gluten, dairy, nuts and seafood, so we can't guarantee any dish is free from cross-contamination.

Our story

A little slice of Spain

We wanted a little piece of that Spanish spirit in Graceville. We opened on Honour Ave in May 2019, stripped the brick walls back and built a bar of handmade deep-green tiles.

We're a small, local team who believe hospitality is about friendship, family and community. Come say hi, we'd love to meet you.

As featured in Broadsheet · The Weekend Edition · Must Do Brisbane · Concrete Playground

Meet the owner

Hola, I'm Maddison

I started out waitressing at a local café while I was at university, then went to help Mum at Hunter & Scout next door shortly after graduating. Safe to say I fell in love with hospitality. So instead of pursuing my Bachelor of Nursing, in 2019 I opened Botellon.

Graceville has always been home. I went to school down the road and know a lot of the local community. Botellon was born out of many conversations about wanting more nightlife in the area, and it's become a local favourite ever since.

These days I'm also building Botellon's new little sibling, Enzo's Pasta & Pizza, right here on Honour Ave.

I film it all on my weekly vlog: the busy Saturday nights, the menu tastings, the tile choices and the bits that don't go to plan.

Good to know

Do I need to book?

We recommend it, especially Friday to Sunday. Walk-ins are welcome when there's room.

Can you cater for dietaries?

Yes. Each dish on our menu is marked with its dietary info, and the team can help on the night. Please note any allergies on your booking.

Are dogs welcome?

Yes, we're dog friendly! Please note your dog on your booking. Dogs need to stay on the ground.

Are kids welcome?

Of course! We don't have a children's menu, but there's plenty on ours to share. For large bookings, kids need to be included in the banquet, and two children under 10 count as one adult. Let us know on your booking if you'll need a high chair.

Where do I park?

There's street parking on Honour Ave, Richmond St and Rakeevan Rd, and Graceville Station is right across the road.

Is it wheelchair accessible?

Yes. Let us know on your booking and we'll set you up with the best table.

Do you do takeaway?

Yes, for pickup. Call us on 0428 121 003 to place your order.

Are there surcharges?

10% on Sundays and 15% on public holidays.

Why do you close at 9.45pm?

We have neighbours close by, so we close at 9.45pm sharp. The kitchen closes at 8.30pm and last drinks are at 9.20pm.
